Questions

The finer details.

How do I subscribe?

Choose a ritual, select your commitment period and preferences, then tap the WhatsApp button. Shirlee's Essentials will receive a structured message with your selection.

Can I choose my favourite scent?

Yes. The order builder lets you select Rain Bath, Pampering Vanilla, Rich Fruits or Caramel Drizzle. Candle scent can also be selected where a candle is included.

Can I choose monthly, quarterly, half-year or annual?

Yes. The builder supports all four commitment periods. The displayed commitment total is the monthly subscription price multiplied by the selected number of months.

What happens after I send the WhatsApp order?

Shirlee's Essentials can confirm availability, payment, delivery or collection arrangements and any final subscription terms directly with you.
